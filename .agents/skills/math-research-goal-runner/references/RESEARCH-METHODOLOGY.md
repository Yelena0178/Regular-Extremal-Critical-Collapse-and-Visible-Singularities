# Autonomous research methodology

## Obligation graph

Work backwards from the exact theorem. Give each node a stable ID, role (NECESSARY, AUXILIARY, SCREEN, EXPLORATORY), precise statement, premises, dependency IDs, scope, evidence, obstacle, verification method and which acceptance clause it advances. Edges mean logical use, not topical similarity. Trace necessary nodes to the final theorem; record alternative routes as explicit AND/OR branches. Reject circular support; splitting a theorem into mutually assumed lemmas adds no evidence.

Choose work by criticality, expected reduction of mathematical uncertainty, feasibility and cost. State the critical missing input and the result that would change the route. Do not equate graph size or node counts with theorem progress.

## Produce mathematics in each useful cycle

Select a concrete target, derive an argument or compute a discriminating witness, audit it, then update only changed records. Suitable outputs include:

- A lemma with a full proof, constants, quantifiers and domain of validity.
- A PDE estimate with bootstrap start/improvement, continuation criterion, parameter dependence, boundary/center terms and derivative accounting.
- An existence/continuous-dependence construction in the stated topology, with constraint and gauge compatibility.
- A geometric or causal argument that identifies the actual solution and boundary before invoking event-horizon conclusions.
- A counterexample, restricted-class impossibility theorem, or explicit obstruction to a proposed proof step.
- A literature interface with exact theorem locator, source version, full assumptions and an assumption-to-project mapping. Retrieve the primary paper; search snippets alone do not establish a theorem. Mark unavailable hypotheses unverified.
- Symbolic or certified computational evidence with exact input and scope, error model, reproducible commands and negative controls. Exploratory computation is useful when it changes a candidate or tests an assumption.

A cycle may legitimately fail. Record the attempted derivation and precise failure; do not manufacture a lemma to satisfy an output quota. A plan alone is not research progress when a feasible mathematical step remains.

## Change routes when evidence warrants it

Group equivalent failures by signature (missing uniform bound, incompatible center condition, unverified causal identity, same failed candidate mechanism). After two materially equivalent attempts, diagnose before repeating: can a stronger estimate fix it, must the candidate change, or is a counterexample likely? This is a route-change trigger, not a runtime blocked-status rule.

Try a genuinely different estimate, coordinate/gauge with justified conversion, compactness/contradiction argument, candidate family or theorem interface. Record why it attacks the failure and a stopping criterion. Further precision is justified only if it can resolve a specified uncertainty and the numerical/analytic error budget supports that use.

Distinguish disproving one route, excluding a specified data class, disproving a universal claim and refuting the original existence theorem. A class obstruction must retain its additional assumptions. If the goal remains open, preserve it and continue with a viable alternative frontier.
